Good to know

Straight answers

Something else? Ask before you buy by replying to any of our emails.

Are these plans stamped / permit-ready?

No. They are complete concept / design plans: dimensioned plans, elevations, sections, details and design-intent foundation, electrical, plumbing and HVAC sheets. A licensed drafter or engineer in your area reviews them, adds what your county requires and stamps them where needed. The CAD files make that quick.

Can my drafter change the plan?

Yes. The floor plan comes as an editable DXF: walls, doors, windows and fixtures on their own layers, with real dimension objects, plus versions with the slab, power, plumbing and HVAC layers. Every other drawing comes as DXF for reference. Or choose Plan + Changes and we make one round of edits.

Is the structure and energy code covered?

As design intent, yes: sheet S0 shows the frames, beams and braced walls the house needs (your engineer sizes them), sheet G2 lists the IECC 2021 insulation and window values for every climate zone, and the details include foundations for cold climates. Your local engineer and building department have the final word.

What does it really cost to build?

Each plan shows the Texas basic / mid / high totals from its own Excel workbook, and the calculator re-prices them for your state. They are planning estimates, not quotes: your builder's numbers decide.

Which plan fits my lot?

Every plan states the lot width it needs. The plan finder checks your lot width, bedrooms, office and budget against all plans in about a minute.

What foundation do the plans use?

A slab on grade (design intent on sheet S1). If your site needs a crawlspace or basement, your engineer adapts the foundation; the rest of the set stays the same.

How do I print the plans?

Print the 11×17 set at 100 % at home or at any print shop; each sheet states its scale. The 24×36 set is for your builder and the permit office.

Can I get a refund?

The files download instantly, so sales are final. If a file is missing, damaged or won't open, tell us within 30 days and we fix it or refund you in full. Ask us anything before you buy.

How many houses can I build?

One build per purchase. Share the files with your builder, drafter, engineer, lender and building department for that build.

Are new plans coming?

Yes. New houses are added to the collections over time, each with the same complete kit. People on the free-sample list hear first.
